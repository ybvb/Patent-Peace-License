   PREAMBLE

   This License exists so that people who offer their work to the world
   are left in peace to do so, and so that a higher, mutually agreed
   standard of conducting business and collaboration can be protected in
   a future of democratized and decentralized development, for everyone
   who develops, however they develop.

   It seeks to defeat no one. It asks one thing of everyone: that no
   patent be used against the shared work. It does not punish a person
   who does so; it dissolves the patent so used, and the way back
   remains open. It is offered in the hope that better licenses will grow
   from it and succeed it.
